About Me

Building brands one story at a time.

I'm Sobana, a Social Media Manager at Brand Monk Academy, based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. I help brands turn everyday moments into content people actually stop to look at.

My path into digital marketing started outside a spreadsheet — running a healthy food franchise brand taught me how a good story sells a product before a single ad does. That instinct now shapes every campaign, caption, and content calendar I build.

I hold certifications from HubSpot and Brand Monk, and I bring an economics background to the table — which mostly means I care as much about what content earns as what it looks like.
